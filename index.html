01 / INTRODUCTION

About REDISM

Realms ov Eternal Darkness, abbreviated as RED, is a personal spiritual and magical path founded on 13 July 2026.

Its followers are known as REDISTS. The spiritual philosophy is known as REDISM.

REDISM is polytheistic and centers on VOIDAR, the supreme deity of this practice, alongside other infernal beings.

The path is founded upon dark magical practices, spiritual exploration, knowledge, wisdom, personal discipline and the pursuit of power.

Within REDISM, darkness represents more than the absence of light. It symbolizes hidden knowledge, wisdom, mystery and power.

02 / THE HEAD DEITY

VOIDAR

The Supreme Deity of RED

VOIDAR is the head deity of Realms ov Eternal Darkness.

The name VOIDAR is derived from the combination of Void and Darkness.

Within this practice, VOIDAR is the sacred name used for Satan.

Represents the whole dark universe, eternal and unconquerable power, pure magick and the Creator ov all.

The Darkness

Represents wisdom, knowledge, hidden mysteries and power.

06 / PHILOSOPHY

The REDIST View of Morality

A REDIST does not believe in the idea of absolute good or bad.

Instead, emphasis is placed on the choices individuals make and their responsibility for the consequences.

Every individual must accept responsibility for their own decisions rather than blame others, fate or supernatural forces.

"It is not about good or bad. It is about the choices you make and living with their consequences."

Questions of a REDIST

  • What choice am I making?
  • What consequences may follow?
  • Am I willing to accept responsibility?

07 / SPIRITUAL PRACTICE

The VOID Meditation

The VOID Meditation is a simple practice designed to develop stillness, concentration, reflection and spiritual awareness.

It does not require imagination or visualization.

Preparation

  • Sit comfortably.
  • Choose a quiet environment.
  • Silence unnecessary distractions.
  • Allow your breathing to remain natural.

Step One — Enter the Silence

Close your eyes if comfortable. Notice your breathing without forcing it.

Step Two — Breath of the Void

As you inhale, silently repeat: "VOIDAR."

As you exhale, silently repeat: "Magick through Darkness."

Step Three — Rest in Stillness

Stop repeating the words. Remain quietly attentive to your breathing, sounds and bodily sensations.

Step Four — Closing

Take three comfortable breaths. Open your eyes and record your reflections in your grimoire.

Duration: approximately 10–20 minutes.
